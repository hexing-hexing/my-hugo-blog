# Team Basketball

Do you like to play team basketball? If so, you will see more good passes and assists. The players cooperate and play as a team.

Team basketball is hard, because we need to share the ball. Many good passes create a good opportunity. But one bad pass can ruin it.

Don't you want to see how the tactics work? Choose team basketball. You may see many bad passes and forced shots in the match logs at first. Your team may be less powerful for a period. There are many problems on the team. Then you try to fix them. It is hard, but it is fun.

There is a talented player on the team. He is our center, young and with high potential (10, the highest in this game). His potential in offensive awareness and passing is even higher.

![Wang, a young passing center, in BasketPulse](/images/basket_train_players_wang2.jpg)

 He is fit for team basketball. Matches become different when you have a center who can pass. He can make his teammates better. We will find good shooters for the team.

Come on!
