# The Draft

There is a final draft at the end of each season. Every team can choose a player. There are ready-to-play players or young players with high potential.

The salary of ready-to-play players is much lower than that of players on the market. They only accept a two-season contract, and then they leave and join the market. Young players accept long contracts, and you can renew with them.

So if you need to win more matches, choose ready-to-play players. But young players are the future of the team, especially if you know their potential from the draft reports.

Our team should choose a ready-to-play player, because three key players will leave next season to save money. There is a good center in the draft. He is a low-post scorer. And there are two good power forwards.

Inside players are very popular now. It seems that almost every team needs good big men. There are many good point guards and shooting guards on the market, but fewer good inside players.

Our team's tactics will focus on inside scoring next season, because we will have four or five inside players. I have given contracts to two young inside players on the market. Our young talented player Wang is a center who has good offensive awareness and passing skills. He will have more chances to get the ball and direct the team.
