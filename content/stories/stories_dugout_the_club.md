# The Club

My club was created in October 2026. It is a small club, and I was told I couldn't spend money like a big club right now. That is the advice from the game manual. So it is not the best time to buy players on the market. What do I already own? I think it is the players on my team right now. They are good enough for the league. I just need to focus on the tactics.

Besides the first team, I have a youth team. This is expected, isn't it? I can't wait to see their scouting reports.

The tactical system depends on the players on the team. It is very complex. If we have a lot of good technical midfielders, we need more short passes during matches. If we have a powerful wing-back, we need more attacks down the wings. But the tactical is a whole system. When a player is on the field, we focus on what he can do. Sometimes we also need to focus on what his teammates can do. So we don't need 11 powerful individuals. We need a powerful team.

Our coach is evaluating young players. This needs time (one players per day). So for now, we wait.
