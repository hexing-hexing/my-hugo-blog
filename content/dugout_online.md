# About Dugout-Online

Dugout-Online is a browser-based soccer management game. You play as the manager of a soccer club. The club is small at first. Then it grows during your managerial career. There are different ways to build the club: training, winning matches in the league, upgrading the pitch, hiring a coach, and more. But you cannot spend money like a big club at the beginning.

It is a deep game. Player attributes and tactical settings are complex. So as a soccer simulation game, it gives you the freedom to build a team.

You can find different kinds of players and develop different tactical systems.

[Play Dugout-Online](https://www.dugout-online.com/)

## 1. Is the game free?

Yes, it is free. But you can also buy a premium account in this game. It provides more functions. For example, you can get more historical data about the tactics of all teams. A premium account cannot improve player ability in matches.

## 2. Do I need to spend a lot of time on the game?

It depends on how you play. You can play five minutes a day. But if you want to, you can spend more time reading game logs and researching tactics. There are two matches a week in a league, but you can play private matches if you like.

## 3. How about the match engine?

It is complex enough as a soccer simulation game. Players have many attributes. Tactical settings are realistic. There are logs for every match.

## 4. How about the community?

The community has a long history and many topics about the game. It is still active as of October 2026.
