# About Basketpulse

![basketpulse_mainpage](/images/basketpulse_mainpage.jpg)

Basketpulse is a browser-based basketball management game. You own a basketball team and act as its manager.

You play matches against real managers from all around the world. There are different kinds of teams. Some have strong athletic ability, while others may have better skills. It depends on how they find and train their players.

So can you choose the kind of team you want? You can't choose a whole team, but you can choose a young talented player at the beginning. He is 16 years old with 10 potential (the highest potential in the game). You can also choose the type of potential. The player can be good at shooting, driving to the basket, defending, rebounding, and more. It depends on your choice.

The height of the player is chosen by you. It usually decides his position on the court.

You can find more about the players and tactics of the game from my blog. I hope you will like it.

[Play Basketpulse](https://www.basketpulse.com/)

## 1. Is the game free?

Yes, it is free. But you can also buy coins in this game. Coins help you analyze a player's potential or analyze a match. You can do the same thing with your own experience and knowledge of basketball, without coins.

Coins do not directly improve player skills during matches.

## 2. Do I need to spend a lot of time on the game?

It depends on how you play. You can play five minutes a day. But if you want to, you can spend more time reading game logs and finding players in
the market.

## 3. How about the match engine?

You can set tactics for individual players, such as their offensive role, shooting rights, and defensive intensity. You can also set tactics for the whole team, such as offensive and defensive tendencies, game tempo, and rebounding effort.
