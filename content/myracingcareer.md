# My Racing Career

My Racing Career is a browser-based racing management game. You manage a racing team, hire a racing driver, create training plans, find sponsors, and hire engineers and coaches.

The racing driver competes in races. He begins with F4 races. The highest level is F1. The car should be tested and set up before the race, including the rear wing, gear ratios, and other settings.

There are many tracks in this game, Some famous tracks have different versions.

[Play My Racing Career](https://app.myracingcareer.com/)

## 1. Is the game free?

Yes, it is free. But you can also buy coins in this game. Coins help you join some private races. Coins do not help you win races directly.

## 2. Do I need to spend much time on the game?

It depends on how you play. You can play five minutes a day. But if you want to, you can spend more time reading race logs and researching tracks.

## 3. How about the match engine?

The engine is very realistic. There are practice sessions where you can set up your car, rear wing, gear ratios, and other settings. Then comes qualifying, and then the main race. You need to develop a strategy for each of these sessions.

Weather, different tracks, engineers, the driver's skills and experience, and your own goals all shape your strategy. How to use the tires and engine, when to attack and when to defend, whether to be aggressive or conservative. The number of pit stops, whether to repair the car, and so on.
